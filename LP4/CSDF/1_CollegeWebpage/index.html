About the Institute

Sunrise Institute of Technology was established in 1998 to provide quality technical education. The institute is approved by AICTE and accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC. More than 3,200 students study in six departments, guided by 180 faculty members, 40% of whom hold a Ph.D.
