About Threadline

A small Pune workshop that grew into a national brand, without giving up on how clothes are made.

Our Story

Threadline was started in 2014 by textile designer Meera Kulkarni and engineer Arjun Deshpande with six tailors in a rented hall in Kothrud. Their idea was simple: well-made basics in honest fabrics at fair prices. Today the company runs two stitching units, 38 stores and an online shop.

Mission and Vision

Mission

Make durable, comfortable clothing that is fairly made and fairly priced.

Vision

Be India's most trusted everyday clothing brand by 2030.

Values

Quality, transparency, sustainability and respect for every worker.
